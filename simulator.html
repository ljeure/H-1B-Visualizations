Base shape. The underlying distribution is FY2024's real annualized-wage histogram for the 92,110 filed petitions, in $10K bins from $0 to $300K+. The slider scales this shape proportionally — it does not change its relative wage mix.

Wage-level quartiles are a proxy, not DOL's real methodology (which weights by occupation and metro area). Levels are fixed at FY2024's own wage quartiles: Level I <$82K, II $82–98K, III $98–126K, IV >$126K.

Weighted and level-based selection are computed as expected-value proportional allocations across bins, not a discrete random draw of individuals — the chart shows the distribution a weighted lottery would produce on average, not one simulated outcome.

$100K consular fee: the ~50/50 domestic/consular split is real, computed directly from FY2024's `REQUESTED_ACTION` field (46,190 consular vs 45,797 already-in-the-US change of status) — assumed to hold across wage and occupation, which is a simplification.

$103K fee: below-$103K demand assumed to go to zero entirely. Above $103K, the reduction percentage removes entries starting from the lowest earners in that remaining pool first, not proportionally across the whole distribution — modeling a fee that prices out the marginal, lower-value sponsorships first.
